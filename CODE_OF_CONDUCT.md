# Code of Conduct - Logihertz Systems (OPC) Private Limited

## Purpose & Core Values

Logihertz Systems (OPC) Private Limited is committed to providing a secure, welcoming, and productive environment for all participants, maintainers, and contributors to `lh-uboot-fuse-tool`.

We prioritize:
- Mutual respect, professional courtesy, and constructive technical discourse.
- Safety, technical rigor, and zero-defect quality in mission-critical hardware root-of-trust tooling.
- Active listening and objective peer review across all skill and experience levels.

## Unacceptable Conduct

The following behaviors will not be tolerated in any project communication or repository space:
- Harassment, discriminatory language, or abusive rhetoric targeting any individual or group.
- Deliberate trolling, insults, or disruptive behavior during issue discussions or pull request reviews.
- Public or private disclosure of non-public personal information without explicit authorization.
- Publication of unsafe or malicious hardware fuse tampering instructions designed to destroy or brick hardware.

## Reporting & Enforcement

Instances of inappropriate conduct may be reported directly to project leadership:
- **Contact**: Sameer Tawade <ops@logihertz.com>

Project maintainers will review all concerns objectively and take appropriate corrective measures, which may include warnings, temporary moderation, or permanent exclusion from the project organization.
